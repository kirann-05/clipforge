LeonidaDaily Clipper

LeonidaDaily Clipper is a personal tool that turns long videos into short vertical clips. It runs on its owner's own computer.

What it does

  • Transcribes a video, finds the strongest moments and edits them into 9:16 clips with captions and sound.
  • Saves finished clips to the owner's Google Drive.
  • Uploads finished clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Who can use it

LeonidaDaily Clipper is used only by its owner for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.
